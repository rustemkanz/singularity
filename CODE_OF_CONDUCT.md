# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers are expected to keep this project technically rigorous, respectful, and usable for others.

That means:

- discuss ideas, code, and tradeoffs directly
- assume good intent until evidence shows otherwise
- avoid personal attacks, harassment, or exclusionary behavior
- keep review feedback specific and actionable
- respect security, privacy, and organizational boundaries when sharing examples or logs

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, intimidation, or threats
- discriminatory or degrading language
- doxxing or disclosure of private information without permission
- deliberate disruption of project discussions or contribution workflows
- knowingly misrepresenting another contributor's work or intent

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions, issues, comments, or other interactions that violate this code of conduct.

## Reporting

If you encounter a serious conduct problem, report it privately to the project maintainers through the repository security or contact channel that is current at the time.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other project-managed collaboration spaces.
